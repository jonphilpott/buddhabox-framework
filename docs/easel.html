How to

Drag from a filled jack (an output) to a hollow one under a slider. The cable adds amount × output to the slider; dashed cables subtract. Click a cable to set its amount or remove it. Shift-click a switch to step it backwards.

The sequencer only plays a tune when SEQ is patched to Pitch at 100%. MOD into FM or Timbre runs at audio rate; everything else updates 689 times a second.

Save into examples/easel_matrix/, then run python3 tools/gen_presets.py examples/easel_matrix/presets.json and flash -e easel_matrix.
